# Native interaction evidence

Branch: `research/native-interactions`. Native/XCUITest source pinned in runtime headers to `621d241510a0e2860eb4ab92869ed85a4bd4a95c`; replay logs retain observed app/test-binary hashes. No Flutter source changed. Accepted scope is actual control/scroll outcomes only, not full gesture dynamics, private ownership, or holdout parity.

## Conclusions and evidence boundaries

- iOS26.4.1/23E254a and iOS27.0/24A434 simulators, iPhone17Pro/iPhone18Pro at402×874@3: four source-pinned ten-trial cohorts per OS accepted,80 trials total. Exact OS/device/environment records and manifest SHA256s are indexed in `interaction_measurements.json`. Every accepted cohort requires exactly ten distinct trial/run/attempt IDs, identical OS/device/config/environment, complete v2 metadata, null reasons, actual delivered touches/pan/offset observations and explicit terminal completion. Caller-declared acceptance alone cannot pass.
- Actual nonmodal test: the same observed presenter-control point is tapped at medium, large, then medium. All accepted nonmodal trials activate the real background UIButton at medium, do not activate it at large, then activate it again at medium. Each probe retains delivered region touches and hit-view IDs; barrier alpha is not the oracle. This is one control/point/configuration, not a universal hit-test mask.
- With `prefersScrollingExpandsWhenScrolledToEdge=true`, the upward content drag starts at offset0, ends at offset0, and native selection changes medium→large. With false, native selection remains medium and content offset increases. Inertia and delivered trajectories vary; do not promote a single final offset or velocity as a native constant.
- The accepted downward recipe on each OS starts at large with public content offset400, drags within the scroll surface, and ends at offset0. Sampled sheet top remains62pt during the probe; native selection stays large. Scroll bounce reaches negative offsets. This recipe does **not** demonstrate within-gesture downward scroll-to-sheet transfer; it falsifies that particular simple handoff hypothesis, not all possible handoff behavior.
- Private `scroll_owner` remains null with a reason. `observed_movement_consumer` is derived from consecutive offset/position changes, not private ownership. `underlying_hit_test` stores the last completed actual probe result, not a continuously measured hit-test mask. Finger velocity is finite-differenced from actual UITouch timestamps/positions; public UIScrollView pan velocity is retained separately.
- Across the80 accepted discrete-outcome traces, QC retains15 unavailable sheet-y frames,22 zero-top frames and a worst inter-frame gap of4.739113s. These traces are **not** accepted timing trajectories. No anomalous sample is rewritten or accepted as an iOS27 motion feature. Full release/overdrag/transition-law calibration is unresolved; it needs reliable sampling and a recipe that actually transfers movement before expansion into more dynamics cases.

## Failed hypotheses and runtime blockers

- Synchronous durable flushing as the repeated~75ms replay delay: falsified by retained diagnostic (max synchronize1.3203ms). Two clocks agree that coalesced callbacks were late. Strict zero-requested-leeway timers anchored to one command boundary remove the repeated delay in ten trials/OS. See `TIMING_DIAGNOSIS.md` and exact timing manifests. No Flutter compensating delay or private spring promotion.
- XCTest success alone as proof: false. iOS26 downward attempt1 trial1 (`2D2F207E-20F9-4253-B23A-CBF0B6730FDE`) began then cancelled without actual delivered moved-touch samples; offset stayed400 despite XCTest passing. Cause of cancellation remains unresolved. Its complete ten-artifact attempt is preserved as failed. The unchanged full ten-trial attempt2 passes actual delivery gates; no individual favorable-trial substitution.
- Cached XCTest runner metadata: detected when iOS26 emitted unresolved source headers and omitted the new fourth test. Those traces remain failed-provenance diagnostics; final collection uses an explicitly selected isolated source-pinned test bundle.
- vPhone iOS26.6.2/23G90 supports install/launch/tree/files APIs, but both point and pixel tap requests returned API success without observed UIKit input/control activation. The own harness was stopped; failed bounded raw prefix and full guest-file location/size are preserved in `artifacts/native/interactions/vphone_nonmodal_delivery_failure/manifest.json`. The bounded prefix cannot prove absence in the unarchived tail. Virtual-device interaction parity remains unresolved; do not create ten fake accepted trials.
