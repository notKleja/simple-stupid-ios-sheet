# iOS 26 to iOS 27 sheet differences

This comparison is limited to repeated opaque native cohorts in
`native_reference/measurements.json`. It does not generalize to unmeasured
devices, configurations, or interactive behavior.

| Finding | Classification | iOS 26 | iOS 27 | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 402×874 portrait page rest | identical | medium `(8, 415.026534, 386, 450.973466)`, large `(0, 62, 402, 812)` | identical values | 10 trials per OS, zero resting spread |
| System medium detent in accepted cohorts | identical | `0.56 × maximum` | `0.56 × maximum` | Phone, iPad, and measured form cohorts |
| Floating page transformation | identical in matched phone rest | 8 pt side/bottom spacing and uniform whole-sheet scaling | same | Resting frames only; transition trajectory unresolved |
| iPad page absolute maximum | measurably different but confounded | 1143 pt, safe top/bottom 32/25 | 1158 pt, safe top/bottom 32/20 | Safe-area cohorts differ, so this is not isolated OS semantics |
| Regular-width form with preferred 320×320 | behavioral difference | maximum 330 pt, medium 184.8 pt, centered x 257 | maximum 1158 pt, medium 648.48 pt, centered x 257 | 10 valid sorted/v2 trials per OS |
| Form leading placement | behavioral difference | unavailable in the measured API/runtime | x 25 pt | iOS 27 leading-placement cohort |
| Programmatic animation object sample | identical observation, unresolved generality | mass 1, stiffness 333.3333332805039, damping 36.51483716411749, duration 0.5058237871186482 s | same sampled family | Not a universal release-physics claim |
| Full transition trajectory | unresolved | recorder gaps retained | gaps plus one-frame teardown observation retained | No frames trimmed to manufacture a pass |
| Gesture, nonmodal touch, scroll, keyboard, stacking, accessibility | unresolved | unmeasured | unmeasured | Required future experiments |

There is no usable iOS 27 vPhone in the current inventory. iOS 27 evidence is
therefore simulator-only; iOS 26 additionally includes a 430×932 vPhone cohort.
